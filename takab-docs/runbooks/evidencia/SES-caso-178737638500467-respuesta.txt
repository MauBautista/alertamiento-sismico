Thank you for the follow-up. Below is the detail you asked for, in the same order.

SENDING FREQUENCY
Very low and event-driven. This is a development and pilot deployment: on the order
of tens of messages per month today. There is no scheduled or bulk sending at all —
every message is triggered by a specific event in a specific building. During an
earthquake we expect a short burst addressed only to the responders registered for
the affected buildings, not a broadcast.

HOW WE MAINTAIN RECIPIENT LISTS
Each institutional customer (a hospital, a university, a civil protection agency)
enters their own recipients in their own console: their staff and their assigned
structural inspectors. We never buy, rent or import lists, and there is no public
subscription form. Recipients are tied to a specific building, so a person only ever
receives mail about the buildings they are responsible for.

HOW PEOPLE STOP RECEIVING MAIL
The customer removes or deactivates the recipient in their console, effective
immediately. Every message we send also states this in its footer. In addition, any
address that hard-bounces or files a complaint is added to the account-level
suppression list and receives no further attempts.

HOW WE MANAGE BOUNCES AND COMPLAINTS
The sending domain (takabailert.com, verified with Easy DKIM RSA-2048, custom MAIL
FROM at bounce.takabailert.com with its own MX and SPF, DMARC published at p=none
while we collect reports) uses a configuration set with:
  - the account-level suppression list enabled for BOUNCE and COMPLAINT;
  - reputation metrics enabled;
  - an event destination publishing BOUNCE, COMPLAINT, REJECT, RENDERING_FAILURE and
    DELIVERY_DELAY to an SNS topic subscribed by our on-call address,
    ops@takabailert.com (subscription confirmed).
Every send is recorded in our database with its recipient, its outcome and the
incident it belongs to, so a message that was accepted but not delivered is visible
rather than assumed.

EXAMPLES OF THE EMAIL WE SEND
There are exactly three kinds. All are plain text, in Spanish, addressed to a named
person who is responsible for a specific building. They carry no images, no tracking
pixels, no promotional content and no unrelated links.

These are generated by the system itself, not written for this reply. In this
deployment they contain no clickable link: our operations console is reachable only
from our own network, so instead of offering a URL the recipient cannot open, the
message tells them where to act. A link is included only where the console is
reachable by the recipient.
