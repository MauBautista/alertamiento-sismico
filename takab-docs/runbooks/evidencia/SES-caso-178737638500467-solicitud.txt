TAKAB Ailert is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for earthquake early warning, structural
monitoring and post-earthquake operational continuity, serving civil protection agencies,
hospitals, universities, industrial sites and corporate campuses in Mexico. Each
institutional customer installs an on-premise gateway in their building and manages their
own recipients from their own console.

MAIL TYPE: transactional only. We send no marketing, newsletters or promotional mail.

WHAT WE SEND. Three kinds, each triggered by a system event:
1. Seismic incident notifications to the responders the customer designated for that
   building.
2. Post-earthquake habitability assessment requests and results, sent to the assigned
   inspector and to the person responsible for the building.
3. Operational alarms (gateway offline, missing backup, stalled queue) to our own on-call
   team.

HOW WE COLLECT ADDRESSES: the institutional customer enters them in their own console, for
their own staff and inspectors. We do not buy, rent or import lists. There are no bulk
imports and no public subscription forms.

HOW RECIPIENTS STOP RECEIVING MAIL: the customer removes or deactivates the recipient in
their console, effective immediately. In addition, any address that bounces or complains is
added to the account-level suppression list and receives no further attempts.

BOUNCE AND COMPLAINT HANDLING. The sending domain uses the `takab-dev-correo` configuration
set, with:
- the account-level suppression list enabled for BOUNCE and COMPLAINT;
- reputation metrics enabled;
- an event destination publishing BOUNCE, COMPLAINT, REJECT, RENDERING_FAILURE and
  DELIVERY_DELAY to an SNS topic subscribed by our on-call address
  (ops@takabailert.com, subscription confirmed).
Every send is recorded in our database with its recipient, its outcome and the incident it
belongs to.

AUTHENTICATION: the domain takabailert.com is verified with Easy DKIM (RSA 2048); we use a
custom MAIL FROM domain at bounce.takabailert.com with its own MX and SPF records; DMARC is
published at p=none while we collect aggregate reports (rua=dmarc@takabailert.com), so we
can tighten the policy based on data rather than assumption.

VOLUME: this is currently a development and pilot deployment, on the order of tens of
messages per month. It grows with each building we onboard. We do not expect bulk sends,
but short bursts during an earthquake, addressed only to the responders for the affected
buildings.
